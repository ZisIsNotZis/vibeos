Which language is best for you?

There is no single winner — choose by the kind of work you want to do.

Popularity depends on the measure. Python, JavaScript, and Java are consistently among the most widely used across surveys and developer communities.
Python is often a comfortable first choice because its syntax is readable and it has a broad ecosystem. The best choice also depends on your goals.
Learning how to solve problems, read documentation, and build projects matters more than choosing a perfect first language.
